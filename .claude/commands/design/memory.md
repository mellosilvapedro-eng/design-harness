Memory: **$ARGUMENTS**

Load the `design-memory` skill.

**No argument, or `show`**: report the store: memories by type, the index, anything
stale or contradictory. With a topic, show what is known about it.

**`consolidate`**:

- **Merge duplicates** into the older file.
- **Promote on repetition**: three occurrences make a rule. Say which.
- **Promote across contexts**: a rule that held in two contexts moves to `memory/taste/universal/`.
- **Verify coordinates** in `memory/sources/`. Correct or remove what does not resolve.
- **Prune** what is contradicted or obsolete. Delete rather than soften.
- **Rebuild `memory/INDEX.md`** to match what exists; check every line points at a real file.

Report what you merged, promoted and deleted, and why, so the user can push back.
